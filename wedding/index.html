The Craft

Shaped by Craft

Every Silvano garment begins with the fabric. In a private consult, you choose from premium cloths, buttons and details, and we take precise measurements.

Each piece is then cut and constructed around you, so it fits cleanly, moves naturally and is made to last.

The Look

Composed for the Groom and His Party

From the groom's tuxedo to every groomsman beside him, each suit is made for the man who wears it, so the whole party looks considered together.

Every detail is chosen with intention, and nothing is in excess.

The Silvano Process

Clear From the First Appointment

Three unhurried appointments, so you always know what comes next.

  1. 01

    Consultation & Measurements

    We talk through what you need and how you will wear it. Together we choose your fabric, style and details, then take precise measurements.

  2. 02

    First Fitting & Adjustments

    We check fit, balance and comfort, then refine the garment so it moves properly with you.

  3. 03

    Final Fitting & Delivery

    Every adjustment is complete. We review the finished garment with you for fit, comfort and appearance, then it is ready to wear.
